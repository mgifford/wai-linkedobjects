# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone who participates in this project. Participation is open to individuals of all backgrounds, identities, and experience levels.

We value diverse perspectives and believe that respectful collaboration leads to better technical outcomes. All contributors, maintainers, and participants are expected to uphold this Code of Conduct in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.

## Our Standards

The following standards apply to all project interactions.

### Be Respectful

- Treat others with dignity and courtesy.
- Engage in constructive discussion, even when you disagree.
- Use inclusive and neutral language.
- Respect differences in experience, background, and opinion.

### Be Professional

- Focus on the technical merits of ideas rather than individuals.
- Provide feedback that is specific, actionable, and grounded in evidence.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and be open to correction.
- Avoid inflammatory, dismissive, or hostile language.

### Be Considerate

- Be mindful of how your communication may be interpreted.
- Assume good faith unless there is clear evidence otherwise.
- Support new contributors and help them understand project norms.
- Respect the time and effort of others.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, or exclusionary conduct of any kind.
- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Trolling, baiting, or deliberate disruption of discussions.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others’ private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Sustained disruption of project discussions, reviews, or workflows.
- Retaliation against individuals who report concerns.

This list is not exhaustive. Maintainers may determine that other conduct is inappropriate if it undermines the safety, inclusiveness, or integrity of the project.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- Clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.
- Setting the tone for respectful and professional collaboration.
- Reviewing reports of misconduct promptly and fairly.
- Taking appropriate corrective action when standards are violated.
- Protecting the privacy and security of individuals who report concerns.

Maintainers have the authority to remove, edit, or reject contributions and to restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including but not limited to:

- GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, and discussions
- Project documentation and related communication channels
- Virtual or in-person meetings associated with the project

It also applies in public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community.

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it using the contact information below.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly. Maintainers will evaluate incidents based on severity, frequency, and impact. Confidentiality will be respected to the extent possible.

Possible consequences include:

1. Informal warning and clarification of expectations.
2. Formal warning.
3. Temporary suspension of participation.
4. Permanent removal from the project community.
5. Additional actions deemed appropriate to protect the project and its contributors.

Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith will not be tolerated.

## Reporting

To report a violation of this Code of Conduct, please contact:

- GitHub: Open a private security advisory or contact a maintainer directly.
- Email: mike.gifford@civicactions.com

When reporting, include as much detail as possible so the situation can be properly assessed.
